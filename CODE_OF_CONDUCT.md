# DecisionForge code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, inclusive and professional project environment.
Participation must be free from harassment regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable and evidence-based feedback.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's sensitive information.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes and focus on a constructive outcome.
- Preserve the integrity of tests, security controls and engineering evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly falsified technical
or security evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting
platform. The owner may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or
ban a participant when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the
DecisionForge project in public spaces.
